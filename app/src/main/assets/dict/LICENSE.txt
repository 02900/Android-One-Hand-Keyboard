Autocorrect dictionaries (es.tsv, en.tsv)

Derived from FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
(content/2018/es/es_50k.txt and content/2018/en/en_50k.txt, word frequencies computed
from OpenSubtitles 2018: http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php)

The source data is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International (CC BY-SA 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes: non-alphabetic tokens and stray single letters removed; Spanish words without an
accent removed when the accented spelling is far more frequent (common misspellings such as
"tambien"); regular forms of the listed Spanish verbs added ("eliminaba", "eliminarlo"),
kept only when the full word list below has them;
English contractions restored ("don't", "I'm"). See tools/build_dictionary.py.
These derived files are distributed under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.

Spanish word set (es_words.bin)

A Bloom filter (tools/build_word_set.py) over a full Spanish word list without accents
(639,292 forms) from an open-source Scrabble project, as included in
https://github.com/02900/boggle (public/diccionario-espanol.txt). It only tells whether a
word exists; it is distributed under that list's open-source terms.
